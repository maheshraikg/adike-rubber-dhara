Privacy policy — ಗೌಪ್ಯತಾ ನೀತಿ

Adike–Rubber Dhara shows arecanut and rubber prices. Farmers use it without an account.

What we store

  • Price alerts / wrong-rate reports (optional): an anonymous Firebase user id, the device's notification token, and the alert settings (crop, variety, market, price). Used only to send the alert. Deleting the alert in the app deletes this record.
  • Partners (co-operatives/traders, optional): Google account email, business name, phone and address they submit, and the rate messages/photos they send. Photos are deleted after the rates are read; the extracted text is kept for audit.

What stays on your phone

Settings, favourite markets and the sales diary (ಮಾರಾಟ ದಾಖಲೆ) are stored only on the device and never uploaded.

Services

Google Firebase (authentication, database, notifications), GitHub Pages (price files), MET Norway (weather; the request contains only the market's rounded coordinates). Rate text from public sources and partner messages may be processed by Google Gemini to read the numbers; no personal data is sent.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions, email the developer (see the Play Store listing or GitHub profile).
